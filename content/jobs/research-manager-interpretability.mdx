## About the role

As a manager on the Interpretability team, you will support researchers and engineers investigating how modern large language models work internally.

This is a high-leverage role where you will partner with an individual-contributor research lead to convert deep research ideas into clear execution plans, unblock the team quickly, and raise delivery quality as we scale.

If your main motivation is to contribute as an individual researcher, we encourage you to apply for our research scientist or research engineer openings instead.

## Responsibilities

- Partner with a research lead on direction, project planning, execution, hiring, and team development
- Set and maintain a high bar for execution speed and quality
- Coach team members for career growth and impact
- Improve recruiting and hiring processes for technical roles
- Strengthen collaboration with adjacent research and product teams
- Communicate team progress to leadership and cross-functional stakeholders

## You may be a good fit if you

- Have 2-5+ years of people management experience in technical teams
- Bring a background in machine learning, AI, or a related technical field
- Enjoy coaching, mentoring, and building healthy team culture
- Have strong project management and prioritization skills
- Can lead teams through ambiguity and changing constraints
- Are comfortable learning and discussing advanced technical topics

## Role details

- Location: San Francisco, CA (hybrid)
- Compensation range: $350,000 - $500,000 USD
- Minimum education: Bachelor's degree or equivalent experience
- Visa sponsorship: Considered for strong candidates
